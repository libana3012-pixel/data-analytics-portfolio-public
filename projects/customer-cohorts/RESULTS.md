# Results: who returned in the next month?
All data is fictional. Last observed month: June 2026.

| First purchase month | New customers | Returned in next month | Month-one retention |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| March | 3 | 2 | 66.67% |
| April | 3 | 1 | 33.33% |
| May | 3 | 1 | 33.33% |
| June | 3 | Not observable | Not observable |

### What this means
In the exercise, two of the three customers who first purchased in March came back in April. The data does not prove a cause for the difference between cohorts. With only three customers per cohort, even one person's behaviour changes the percentage substantially.

### A quality decision
June is **not** assigned 0% retention: July data is not available. A missing follow-up period is different from observing no repeat purchases.
